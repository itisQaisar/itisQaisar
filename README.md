## 👋 About me

I'm an **AI researcher with a background in data science, data engineering, and software development**. I completed an **M2 in Data Science at Université Paris-Saclay (2026)** and conducted research at **Cnam Paris - CEDRIC Laboratory (ISID team)**.

## 🔬 Research experience

### Knowledge-Graph-Aware Multimodal Retrieval for Cultural Heritage
**Research Intern · Cnam Paris, CEDRIC (ISID) · March–September 2026**

Research on multimodal retrieval for the *Grains de Culture* cultural-heritage knowledge graph, supervised by **Dr. Nada Mimouni** and **Prof. Jean-Claude Moissinac**.

- Studied **CLIP, TinyCLIP, and MobileCLIP** representations for retrieving artworks through natural-language and visual queries.
- Built and compared retrieval workflows using **FAISS** vector indexes, text/visual representations, and **knowledge-graph-aware reranking**.
- Worked with **RDF, SPARQL, and Apache Jena Fuseki** to incorporate artwork metadata and semantic relationships into retrieval.
- Explored **multimodal fusion** and **GraphRAG-style context enrichment** for more informed search.
- Evaluated approaches using **Hit@1/5/10, mean reciprocal rank (MRR), retrieval latency, and index/resource trade-offs**.
